# **klogs --- Modern Payments & Fraud Intelligence Platform**

Welcome to **klogs**, a next-generation ecosystem offering **Payment
Gateway**, **Payment Orchestration**, **Fraud Intelligence**, **BIN/IIN
Services**, **Instant Currency Exchange**, and **B2B Online Payments**.\
We build secure, scalable, developer-friendly financial services for
merchants, fintechs, and SaaS platforms.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## **Core Products**

### **Payment Gateway**

Our developer-first Payment Gateway simplifies how businesses accept
online payments.

**Capabilities:** - Multi-provider virtual POS support\
- Unified & responsive checkout\
- Payment links (email/SMS)\
- Subscription & recurring billing\
- Advanced analytics, refund/void flows\
- High availability & scalable infrastructure

------------------------------------------------------------------------

### **Payment Orchestration**

A smart orchestration engine designed to improve success rates and
optimize transaction costs.

**Features:** - Intelligent routing\
- Provider failover & retry logic\
- Fee-based smart selection\
- Tokenization and secure card vault\
- Provider performance dashboards

------------------------------------------------------------------------

### **Fraud Detection & Risk Engine**

A real-time, ML-enhanced risk scoring system that protects your business
from fraud while minimizing friction.

**Includes:** - Real-time scoring on every transaction\
- ML-based anomaly detection\
- Velocity checks (IP, device, card, email, behaviour patterns)\
- Device fingerprinting & IP reputation\
- Custom fraud rule DSL\
- Explainable AI (XAI) insights

------------------------------------------------------------------------

### **BIN / IIN Service**

A powerful BIN/IIN lookup service for banks, issuers, and payment
companies.

**What it provides:** - Card scheme (Visa, Mastercard, Troy, etc.)\
- Issuer information\
- Card type (Credit, Debit, Prepaid, Corporate)\
- Country & bank metadata\
- Fast lookups & caching for high-traffic apps\
- API-first, high availability design

------------------------------------------------------------------------

### **Exchange --- Instant Currency Converter**

A high-accuracy, real-time currency conversion API designed for payment
flows.

**Highlights:** - Multi-currency instant calculation\
- Real-time FX rates from global providers\
- Margin configuration for merchants\
- Inline conversion inside payment flows\
- Perfect for marketplaces, PSPs, SaaS platforms

------------------------------------------------------------------------

### **B2B Online Payment Infrastructure**

Enterprise-grade B2B payment solutions built for companies needing
secure, trackable, and automated payment capabilities.

**Includes:** - Link-based B2B payment requests\
- High-limit transaction support\
- Detailed reporting & reconciliation\
- Multi-company (multi-tenant) structure\
- Customizable approval & workflow layers\
- Role-based dashboards for corporate teams
